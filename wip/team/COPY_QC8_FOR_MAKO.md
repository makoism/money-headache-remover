- What is left of Free to spend has four names: "safe to spend", "Left to spend", "Free to spend left", "left".
- "Saved" means what the burrows hold, and "Saved in this browser" at the top means stored numbers.
- The app is "the burrow" on the welcome screen and "the Burrow" on cloud lines.
- Home calls Education, Giving and Investing burrows. Plan has "Goal burrows" with only the goals.
- "Left over" in the Ask table is the same money as "Not placed yet".
- The backup button has three names: "Save file", "Save a copy", "Save a file instead".
- Loading the cloud copy: "Load", "Load it", "Load the newer copy".
- "Keep mine" and "Keep what is here" mean the same.
- "Use it on another device" is five words for a button. "OK" on the Numbers restored notice names no action. "Put it toward the goal" does not say which goal.
