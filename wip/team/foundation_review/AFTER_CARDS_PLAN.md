## The fault (qc-north-star on candidate 2, blocker 1)
The figure runs from the earliest unpaid card payment (D1) to the first counted pay landing after D1, and takes only the card payments in that stretch (lookahead.js, "each bank after its cards are paid", BK3, BK5). Made-up yen, set up 24 Nov: bank 168,000, pay 300,000 on the 10th, Card A 54,000 paid on the 10th, Card B 23,000 on the 11th: 391,000 after 10 and 11 Dec, right. Add Card C 12,000 paid on the 27th: D1 is 27 Nov, the first pay after it lands 10 Dec, so the stretch ends 10 Dec and Card B on 11 Dec is left out: 402,000 "on 27 Nov and 10 Dec". A new debt of 12,000 raised the figure by 11,000. Right is 379,000 after 27 Nov, 10 Dec and 11 Dec. For Mako's own two cards today the figure is right; it breaks the day a card paid before the pay is added.

## The rule
Per bank: **one next unpaid payment for each card paid from that bank** (an overdue one is taken today, as now), and the day is the **latest of those days**. The figure starts from the bank today, adds the counted pays landing on or before that day (a pay on the day itself counted first, the walk's order), and takes off bills, moves, loan parts and those card payments up to that day. Never plan lines, never goals. Every card payment the walk holds up to that day is taken off and its day listed. The walk for this figure runs to that latest day even when it is past the usual horizon, so the day and the bills taken are true for a card whose next payment is far out (qc-foundation finding 1). A card payment day inside the stretch that has no amount yet (a "not sure" card's payment after its next one) cannot be taken off: it is named in a new answer field, `laterCardDays: [{ card, day }]`, so Home and the assistant can say so, never silently left out (finding 2). A late or not seen pay is still not counted and is named (BH1). The dip before the day, the money set aside, and "close days not sure" are worked out as now, over the new stretch.
