# Your first run with the real assistant

On your Android phone, on the live link. About 10 minutes. Use these made-up amounts, not your real ones.

Before you start: open the link. The first screen says hi and asks which country you bank in. Type in the box at the bottom and tap Send. Say one thing at a time, as below.

The assistant may word its questions its own way. That is fine. If it asks something you already said, say it again.

## What to say, and what you should see

1. **"Japan, yen"**
   You see "Saved: Country" and a card "Your answers so far".

2. **"One bank, Main bank. It shows 286,000"**
   You see "Saved: Your bank". The card says Main bank: ¥286,000 on today's date.

3. **"Once a month on the 10th. The day before if it's a weekend"**
   It asks how much lands in your bank.

4. **"240,000, after tax"**
   You see "Saved: Pay". The card says Pay: ¥240,000 after tax, on day 10 each month.

5. **"No side job"**
   It asks about credit cards.

6. **"Card A has 54,000, paid in full on the 10th. Card B has 23,000, paid on the 11th. Not sure about close days"**
   You see "Saved: Cards". Both cards are on the card.

7. **"Rent 72,000 on the 27th, power 7,400 on the 26th"**
   You see "Saved: Bills". If it asks whether a bill already came out this month, say yes or no.

8. **"Not sure about everyday spending yet, and no minimum"**
   You see "Saved: Free to spend" and "Saved: Keep at least". It asks if it should fill in your Burrow. The card has a button "Fill in my Burrow".

9. **"Yes, fill it in"**
   You see a card "Filled in" with Safe to spend and Bank today ¥286,000, and an Undo button. Home shows Main bank ¥286,000.

10. **"I spent 1,200 on lunch from Main bank"**
    You see a card "Changed": "Log ¥1,200 on Free to spend from Main bank, today (lunch)", with before and after, and Undo. Home shows Main bank ¥284,800, and Safe to spend is 1,200 less. Tap Undo: Main bank is ¥286,000 again.

If you leave out "from Main bank", it asks "Card or bank?" once, and remembers your answer for the next spend.

## If something goes wrong

- It says "That didn't go through": tap Send again. One failed message never turns it off.
- It says "The assistant is off right now": tap "Set up on the screens". Your answers so far are filled in there.
- Anything else odd: open Settings, tap "Copy the assistant's error log", and paste it in the coordinator chat. It has no money figures and none of your words.
- Undo is on every card that changed something.
